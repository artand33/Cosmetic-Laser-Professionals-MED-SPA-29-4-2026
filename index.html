Laser Hair Removal

Botox & Dysport

Smooth wrinkles naturally — preventive and corrective treatments

  • Targets forehead lines, crow's feet, and brow furrows
  • Onset in 3–5 days; full effect in 2 weeks
  • Results last 3–4 months on average
  • Minimal downtime — avoid lying flat for 4h post-treatment

Results vary. Consultation required.

Dermal Fillers

Volume, lift, and lip enhancement with FDA-approved HA fillers

  • Lips, cheeks, nasolabial folds, under-eye hollows
  • FDA-approved hyaluronic acid — natural and reversible
  • Lasts 6–18 months depending on area and product
  • Mild swelling possible for 24–48h

Results vary. Consultation required.

Facials & PRP

Medical-grade facials and platelet-rich plasma rejuvenation

  • Options: medical-grade peels, microdermabrasion, anti-acne
  • PRP uses your own growth factors for natural rejuvenation
  • 3–6 PRP sessions for optimal results
  • Mild redness resolves within 24–48h

Results vary. Consultation required.

Body Sculpting

EMSlim, Beautifill, and fat-dissolving injections for real contour

  • Equivalent to 20,000 contractions per session
  • 4 sessions recommended (2 per week)
  • Zero downtime — walk in, walk out

Common Questions

FAQ

Our sapphire cooling tip technology makes laser hair removal virtually painless. Most clients describe the sensation as a light snap — far less discomfort than traditional waxing. Sensitive areas may feel slightly more intense, but our team will adjust settings to keep you comfortable.

Most clients achieve optimal results in 6–8 sessions. Hair grows in cycles, so each session targets a different phase of growth. The exact number depends on your skin tone, hair color, and treatment area. We'll give you a personalized plan during your consultation.

Yes. Our sapphire cooling technology is FDA-cleared for all skin types and tones, including Fitzpatrick types IV–VI. We have extensive experience treating Miami's diverse population. Your provider will assess your skin and customize the treatment parameters at your consultation.

Results typically last 3–4 months. With regular treatments, many clients find their results last longer over time as the targeted muscles gradually weaken. We recommend scheduling a follow-up about 2–3 weeks after your first treatment to assess results.

We require 24-hour notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee. We understand life happens — please contact us as early as possible if you need to change your appointment.

Yes. We accept Cherry Financing and CareCredit, which allow you to split the cost of treatments into monthly payments. Ask our front desk team about current promotional rates — many clients are approved in minutes.

For laser hair removal: shave the area 24h before, avoid sun exposure for 2 weeks prior, and skip any retinol or exfoliants for 48h. For injectables: avoid blood thinners (aspirin, fish oil) for 3–5 days before. We'll send you full prep instructions when you book.

Yes. All treatments at CLP are performed under the medical supervision of our Medical Director, Dr. Otto Marquez Mendoza. Our owners are both licensed paramedics, and our nurse practitioners hold active Florida APRN licenses. You're in qualified hands.
